Privacy Policy

Grid Down HQ is a personal tool used only by its owner, Christopher Czarnecki. It runs on the owner's own computer.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission, it connects to the owner's own YouTube channel (to manage videos and read channel statistics) and the owner's own Google Drive (to store backups of its own files).

How data is used

Data stays on the owner's computer and in the owner's own Google account. It is not sold, shared with anyone else, or used for advertising. No other person's data is collected.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
